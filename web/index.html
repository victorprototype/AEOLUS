AEOLUS

Geometry-conditioned neural quantum Monte Carlo for ranking the stability of molecular conformers and tautomers.

Why AEOLUS

Read the full abstract

Modern drug discovery and materials design depend on computational methods to evaluate molecular stability before committing to costly laboratory synthesis. Researchers currently face a fundamental tradeoff: Density Functional Theory (DFT) is fast but can be unreliable, particularly in capturing electron correlation, while neural-network-based quantum Monte Carlo methods such as FermiNet solve the electronic Schrödinger equation directly, achieving high accuracy but requiring full re-optimization for every individual molecular geometry. This makes FermiNet accurate yet impractically slow when applied across the many conformers and tautomers a single candidate molecule can adopt.

AEOLUS addresses this gap by extending FermiNet with a geometry-conditioned, E(3)-equivariant Graph Neural Network layer. Rather than training a separate model for each configuration, AEOLUS injects geometry-aware embeddings into FermiNet's electron representation, enabling a single trained model to evaluate energies across a continuous space of related molecular geometries. This preserves FermiNet's core physical guarantees — fermionic antisymmetry, rotational and translational equivariance, and correct electron-nucleus cusp behavior — while significantly reducing the computational cost of evaluating multiple conformers.

The core output is a tool for ranking the relative stability of small-molecule conformers and tautomers at near-chemical accuracy, validated against coupled-cluster (CCSD(T)) reference calculations and benchmarked using QM7 for baseline correctness and GEOM/Platinum for conformer-specific evaluation. AEOLUS is positioned not as a replacement for existing discovery pipelines, but as a precision filtering step applied to a shortlist of promising candidates, reducing wasted synthesis effort on unstable structures.

By advancing scientific computational infrastructure (SDG 9), supporting pharmaceutical research and development (SDG 3), and reducing resource waste in early-stage research (SDG 12), AEOLUS contributes to more efficient, sustainable, and accessible scientific discovery.
